Fix your profile first

No photo, no bio, no samples? Clients scroll past. Add a clear photo and a one-line bio.

Then pick a handle that is easy to read. Easy to read means easy to remember. Pin three samples.

Because you are a designer, your work is your resume. Show the real thing.

Add a link to where people can reach you.

03 Notes on
freelancing# Price the
result

Quote by the project

Hourly rates punish you for getting faster. Quote one price for one clear result.

List what is included. List what is not. Write down how many rounds of changes you offer.

A fixed price with clear scope protects both sides.
